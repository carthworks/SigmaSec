1 · Signup, login, session POST /auth/signup · POST /auth/token · GET /auth/me

6 · Live progress GET /scans/{id}/progress (SSE)

The frontend keeps this connection open while a scan runs. It polls Redis every 4 seconds and streams status/percent/log-tail as server-sent events until the scan reaches a terminal state.

  • scans.py:266 get_scan_progress()
    • reads Redis: scan:{id} hash — written by scan_tasks.py:25 update_progress() and scan_tasks.py:38 mark_subtask_done() (10% → 80% as each of nuclei/trivy/gitleaks finishes)
    • reads Redis: scan:{id}:logs list — appended by adapters/runner.py:51 _LogSink inside every adapter subprocess call
    • loop exits once Scan.status is complete or failed
